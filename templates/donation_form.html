Packaging guide for physical supplies

These steps apply when you physically bring or send items to the NGO. The demo checkout only records a simulated contribution; it does not purchase or dispatch goods.

Check packaging and expiry

Food, baby food, and milk powder should be sealed, unopened, in original packaging, and within expiry. Keep items clean and dry.

Group and count items

Group the same product together and count it before sealing. Separate food from non-food supplies, such as torches and tarpaulins.

Pack and label clearly

Use a sturdy, securely closed carton or bag. Write the exact item name, pack size, and quantity on the outside so the team can sort it quickly.

Attach the donation QR label

Register the physical donation, print the QR from your Donation Passport, and attach it to the main box or each parcel as instructed. Keep the code visible and scannable.

Made a packing mistake? Don't worry—if a label is unclear or a quantity needs correction, the team will try to contact you and clarify it before sorting. Unsafe, contaminated, expired, or unsealed food products may need to be set aside or declined for safety.
